# N1 — non-confirmatory event-frequency analysis design

Status: **NON-CONFIRMATORY PROPOSAL, not an added protocol requirement**.
All frozen H1 thresholds, signed paired differences, same-original-position Task14
baseline references, precision floors, runnable off-CPU evidence and controlled
intervention conditions remain unchanged. Three moderate events in two sessions
alone establish neither unusual frequency nor scheduler causation. Ordinary noise,
threshold multiplicity and many opportunities to exceed a threshold matter.

A future approved exploratory report may tabulate event counts AND denominators
per region/protocol/session/original position/variant, retaining signed and negative
differences, all non-events, severe events and precision-invalid/missing metrics as
separate categories. Compare matched no-helper/reference observations with the
predeclared helper controls under identical query/precision rules; show absolute
rates, paired rate differences and session-level heterogeneity. Do not compare
instrumented C/D frequencies as if they were ordinary A population rates. Keep
historical/new estimands separate; do not treat source repetitions as independent.

Use cluster-aware descriptive uncertainty and all predeclared conditions, not
post-hoc threshold tuning, event-count p-values or favorable control selection.
If repeated baseline/reference measurements are needed beyond the frozen schedule,
propose an explicit amendment before collecting them. No such repetitions or new
confirmatory test are adopted here. The existing 80% explained-excess and 50%
intervention heuristics remain qualified engineering rules, not statistical coverage
or automatic cause acceptance. Direct query-linked RUNNABLE scheduler traces,
uncertainty-valid attribution and alternatives remain required; blocked waits,
frequency and on-CPU memory stalls stay distinguishable or UNRESOLVED.
